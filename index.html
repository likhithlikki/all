My Projects

All my websites and repositories in one place. Tap ☆ to pin your favorites.

✏️ Make Changes

Add projects, choose favorites or remove cards here. You will get one code to copy at the end.

1 Add a new project

Fill the boxes and press the button. The card appears on the page right away.

2 Choose favorites or remove

Tick ★ to pin a project at the top. Press Remove to delete a card.

3 Copy your code

Every change above is already inside this one code.

🚀 Open my repo & paste
  1. Press Copy code.
  2. Press Open my repo & paste. It opens projects.js in the editor of your all repo.
  3. Select everything (Ctrl+A), paste the new code (Ctrl+V), then press Commit changes.
  4. Wait about a minute and refresh. Your changes are now on every device.
